## Caption Rules

- Start with the family problem, not the company.
- Every caption should include `carelolo.com` or `(984) 400-4008`.
- Mention `$30/hr` only where it helps conversion.
- Use "non-medical support" when describing scope.
- Use one CTA per post.
- Avoid fear-based language. LoLo should feel calm and capable.
