# LoLo Meta Lead Gen Creative Refresh V2

## CMO critique of the prior set

- Too much low-contrast ivory-on-photo treatment. It looked premium, but not thumb-stopping enough for paid acquisition.
- Copy was too soft in a few places. Paid lead gen needs a fast problem, a clear promise, and one next step.
- Price appeared too often. `$30/hr` is a strong hook, but if every ad says it, the brand can start to feel discount-led instead of trust-led.
- Some layouts felt like organic brand posts instead of direct-response ads.
- The old campaign creatives had urgency and clear phone visibility, but they were now off-brand because of HomeCare HUB, old URL, old colors, and heavy flyer-style blocks.

## What changed

- 10 distinct creative concepts.
- Stronger contrast with evergreen, ivory, and coral.
- One clear idea per image.
- Price appears only in `06-price`.
- All files use `LoLo`, `carelolo.com`, and `(984) 400-4008`.
- Built in both square feed and 4:5 portrait versions.

## Recommended launch order

Start with these 3:

1. `01-this-week`  
   Best direct-response hook. Simple callback promise.

2. `06-price`  
   Use as the price/value challenger. Do not make it the only message.

3. `03-short-visits`  
   Strongest category education: short visits, real relief.

Second wave:

4. `02-mom-fine`
5. `07-one-visit`
6. `10-cant-be-there`

Keep as alternates:

7. `04-real-door`
8. `08-less-worry`
9. `09-evening`
10. `05-no-agency`

## Instant form reminder

Use the same promise as the creative:

- CTA: `Request callback`
- First form line: `Tell us what kind of support your family needs. LoLo will call you back.`
- Ask only: name, phone, ZIP, care need, preferred callback time, optional notes.
