# LoLo Meta Lead Gen Refresh

## What changes now

- Replace all HomeCare HUB creative with LoLo.
- Use `carelolo.com` and `(984) 400-4008` everywhere.
- Keep the campaign family-side only.
- Lead with trust, simple help, clear price, and callback next step.
- Avoid medical visuals, hospital cues, scrubs, or agency-heavy language.

## Creative angles to test

1. `Short visits`: Need help with one thing?
2. `Mom says fine`: When Mom says she is fine...
3. `Clear price`: Home care can start at $30/hr.
4. `Breathing room`: Care for them, breathing room for you.
5. `This week`: Need support this week?
6. `Not an agency`: Trusted help, without the agency feel.

## Recommended instant form

Use a higher-intent form if lead quality is weak. Keep questions short:

- Name
- Phone
- ZIP code
- What kind of help do you need?
- When should we call?
- Optional notes

Confirmation screen:

Title: `Thanks. LoLo will call you back.`

Body: `We received your request. You can also call or text us at (984) 400-4008.`

Button: `Visit carelolo.com`

URL: `https://carelolo.com/get-care`

## Primary text options

### Short visits

Need help with just one thing this week? LoLo helps families arrange non-medical companionship and everyday help for older adults at home. Care starts at $30/hr.

Call or text (984) 400-4008 or request a callback.

### Mom says fine

When an older parent says they are fine, it can still help to have someone nearby. LoLo gives families a simpler way to start trusted home care and companionship.

Care starts at $30/hr. Request a callback today.

### Clear price

Home care should feel clear before you start. LoLo offers non-medical companionship and everyday help, with care starting at $30/hr.

Tell us what your family needs and we will call you back.

### Breathing room

You can love someone and still need backup. LoLo helps families arrange warm, practical support for an older adult at home.

Visit carelolo.com or call/text (984) 400-4008.
