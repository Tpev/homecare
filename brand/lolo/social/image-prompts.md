# LoLo Image Generation Prompts

Use these prompts to create future lifestyle images. Keep the output realistic, warm, and inspection-friendly. Avoid hospital settings, uniforms that look clinical, exaggerated smiles, stock-photo posing, or dark moody lighting.

## Family Hero Image

Create a realistic warm editorial photo for LoLo, a US home care and companionship brand. Scene: an older adult aged 70+ sitting comfortably at home with a kind caregiver nearby, both smiling naturally in conversation. Soft natural daylight, warm ivory and oat interiors, subtle evergreen clothing accent, calm premium feeling. Non-medical home setting, no hospital bed, no medical devices, no stethoscope, no logo, no text, no exaggerated stock-photo pose. Horizontal composition with space for text on the left.
