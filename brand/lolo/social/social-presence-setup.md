## Tone Rules

- Warm but not sentimental.
- Premium but accessible.
- Calm, adult, and clear.
- Use "older adults" more than "seniors."
- Use "family" and "support" more than industry jargon.
- Avoid jokes, hype, and startup slang.

## Content Pillars

- Family relief: fewer stressful unknowns.
- Transparent care: clear starting rate and expectations.
- Companionship: practical, emotional, everyday support.
- Trust: reviewed caregivers and clear process.
- Education: explain non-medical support simply.
- Conversion: request a callback at carelolo.com or call (984) 400-4008.
