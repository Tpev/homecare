# Recurring care audit and proposed changes

September 7, 2026. Proposal only; no application, database, payment, message or scheduling changes. This review combines the existing desktop/mobile screenshots and text/control inventories with read-only source inspection. It is an expert review, not a measured usability test. The [data preservation map](data-preservation-map.md) defines what must survive the redesign.

## Main recommendation

Give a recurring plan one clear home, with a dated visit opening directly into the same visit experience used by one-time care. The plan explains the ongoing agreement; the visit explains what happens on that day. Put the current or next visit and every pending decision ahead of the long background profile. Keep the complete profile, agreement, records and history available in predictable places.

The proposed navigation matches the main proposal. It does not require a new backend record or a new page for every section.

| Level | First view | Supporting destinations | Always available |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recurring request before hire | Overview: replies or invitation state, then one clear next action | Caregivers; Care details; Help, in stable order | Request identity, weekly schedule, public/private visibility and direct Message action when available |
| Recurring plan | Overview: plan status, attention queue, live/next visit, short agreed schedule | Visits; Care details; Help, in stable order | Caregiver, recipient, plan ID, current payment attention and direct Message action |
| Dated recurring visit | Overview: dated visit state, next action and visit record/activity | Caregivers; Care details; Help, in stable order | Date, caregiver, recurring-plan breadcrumb, hours/payment state and direct Message action |

“Visits” belongs to the recurring plan. It contains scheduled and past occurrences, with the existing global Schedule/History remaining useful across all care. A one-time visit does not need an empty Visits tab. Caregivers remains the selection workspace before hire; after hire it shows the selected caregiver first and retains recruitment history. The plan header/Overview links to the relevant caregiver context. Conversations keep their existing records and get reliable direct links rather than a new local tab. Visit events remain in that visit's Overview record; plan-level history stays clearly scoped to the plan.

## Preserve the actual schedule-change contract

| Family intent | Existing effect to retain | Proposed confirmation and result |
|---|---|---|
| Skip one dated visit | Only a scheduled booking can be skipped. Its payment is cancelled/released through the existing service, cancellation/late flags and events are retained, and the recurring plan continues. | Show the exact date/time, current late-window consequence and “Other recurring visits stay scheduled.” Keep the cancelled visit in history. |
| Reschedule one dated visit | Visit-level change request with reason and proposed start/end; current schedule continues pending acceptance. | Title “Request a new time for [date]”; compare old/new times and show waiting status in both visit and plan attention. |
| Change future weekly schedule | Proposed weekday/time slots, effective local date, schedule bounds and note are recorded. On caregiver acceptance, future scheduled bookings from the effective boundary are cancelled and occurrences are materialized under an incremented schedule version; past and earlier visits remain traceable. | Show current/proposed schedule and affected bookings before sending. After acceptance show the effective date and new dates; retain old changes and cancellation history. Do not imply immediate confirmation at send time. |
| Ask for one extra visit | Records an extra-visit proposal; it becomes a booked extra occurrence only after acceptance. The weekly pattern does not change. | Distinguish “Request sent” from “Visit booked.” Display date, timezone, start/end, duration, note and acceptance status. |
| Pause recurring care | From date and optional return date define the pause interval. A future pause leaves the plan active until its start. Qualifying scheduled visits in the interval are cancelled, with dates and reasons recorded. | Show “Pause scheduled from [date]” before the pause starts, then “Paused until [date]” or “Paused until resumed.” List affected dates and the first expected post-pause date without falsely promising a booking. |
| Resume | Existing service restores future cancellations caused by this pause, clears pause fields, materializes/prepares visits and records the event. | Show the actual next scheduled booking after success; if still preparing, say so. Do not restore manually skipped/cancelled visits simply because the UI says Resume. |
| End | Stops new recurring occurrences and cancels future scheduled visits except the earliest one retained by default; family may explicitly cancel that one too. Existing active/in-progress visits and historical financial/reporting work do not vanish. | Show the exact kept/cancelled visit and consequences before confirmation. Afterwards retain actionable visits, outstanding hours/payment/report/dispute items and access to all records. |

Sources: `app/Services/RegularCare/CarePlanService.php` methods `respondToScheduleChange`, `skipVisit`, `pausePlan`, `resumePlan`, `endPlan`; `app/Livewire/Family/RegularCareShow.php`; [recurring source inventory](../recurring-source-inventory.md).

## Useful validation tasks for the redesign

Test the same tasks on desktop and mobile with no explanation: identify whether care is accepted; find today's exact visit; respond to the right payment issue when two visits need attention; skip one visit while retaining the weekly pattern; explain what changes after a new schedule is accepted; pause with a return date; end a plan while keeping its next visit; approve corrected hours without mistaking the old receipt for confirmation; retrieve a cancelled/refunded visit; identify which profile version applied to a past visit.

Success means the family can name the record, state and consequence before acting. The data preservation map is the acceptance checklist for ensuring a shorter first screen does not remove information.
