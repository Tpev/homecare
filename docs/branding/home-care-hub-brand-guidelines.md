# Home Care HUB Brand Guidelines

## Brand essence
- Human + tech hybrid
- Warm, premium, calm, dependable
- Human-centered care with modern clarity
- The product should feel more like peace of mind than like software

## Core message
- Thoughtful care. Smarter by design.
- Technology exists to make care feel more personal, more reliable, and easier to manage.

## Color palette
- Deep Teal: `#0F3D3E`
- Soft Blue: `#4F6FAF`
- Lavender: `#7C5DDC`
- Cream: `#F5F1EB`
- Soft White: `#FAF9F7`

## Recommended usage
- Deep Teal:
  - primary CTA backgrounds
  - headline text
  - dark feature panels
  - trust and safety sections
- Soft Blue:
  - supportive accents
  - secondary data highlights
  - subtle gradients
- Lavender:
  - links
  - secondary CTA accents
  - highlighted words or emphasis
- Cream / Soft White:
  - page backgrounds
  - elevated surfaces
  - form backgrounds

## Typography
- Display / editorial headings:
  - Brand board references "Plus Jakarta Serif"
  - Web implementation should use a warm, readable serif substitute
  - Recommended web substitute: `Source Serif 4`
- UI / body / forms:
  - `Inter`

## Typography rules
- Headlines should feel editorial, calm, and confident
- Avoid shouting startup typography:
  - avoid all-caps headlines
  - avoid forced italics as a default brand move
  - avoid aggressive tracking
- Body copy should be clean, direct, and highly readable

## UI principles
- Rounded, soft surfaces
- Light borders instead of harsh outlines
- Shadows should feel diffused and premium, not heavy
- Cards should feel breathable and reassuring
- Primary actions should be obvious without loud neon color
- Secondary actions should use soft surfaces and lavender/blue accents

## Components
- Primary button:
  - Deep Teal fill
  - Soft White text
  - Rounded corners
  - Subtle depth
- Secondary button:
  - Soft White or Cream surface
  - Light border
  - Teal or Lavender text
- Inputs:
  - Warm white surface
  - Soft border
  - Strong focus ring using Soft Blue / Lavender tint
- Trust row:
  - icon + short proof phrase
  - low visual noise
  - should feel like reassurance, not compliance theater

## Imagery
- Warm natural light
- Caring human interactions
- Older adults shown with dignity, calm, and comfort
- Clean home settings
- Human hands, proximity, and eye contact are strong signals
- Device/product mockups should feel elegant and secondary to the caregiving story

## Mobile-first guidance
- First screen should communicate:
  - what the service is
  - who it helps
  - the first action to take
- Keep primary CTA and first form visible early
- Avoid dense comparison tables or over-stacked metrics on small screens
- Trust elements should support action, not compete with it

## Content tone
- Compassionate, direct, and capable
- Speak like a high-trust service brand, not a generic marketplace
- Focus on clarity, calm, reliability, and relief

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Neon startup blues dominating the experience
- Cold hospital visuals
- Overly technical feature-first copy
- All-caps marketing blocks everywhere
- Dark, heavy black panels that feel severe instead of reassuring
