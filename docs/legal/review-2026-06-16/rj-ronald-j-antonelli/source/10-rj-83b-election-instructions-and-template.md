Recipient: Ronald J. Antonelli  
Company: Lolo Care, Inc.  
Stock: 1,000,000 shares of Common Stock, including 500,000 shares subject to vesting and repurchase rights

## Important Notice

This document is not tax advice. RJ should consult tax counsel immediately. If an 83(b) election is appropriate, it generally must be filed with the IRS no later than 30 days after the restricted stock is transferred.

Missing the 30-day deadline can have serious tax consequences.

## Why an 83(b) Election May Matter

The Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement issues shares at closing, but 500,000 shares remain subject to vesting and company repurchase rights. An 83(b) election may allow RJ to elect to include the value of the restricted shares in income at the time of transfer rather than as the shares vest.
