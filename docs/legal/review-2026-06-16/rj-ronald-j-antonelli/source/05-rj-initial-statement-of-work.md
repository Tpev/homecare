RJ will assist LoLo Care Inc with legal, regulatory, corporate, operational, commercial, and strategic tasks assigned by the Board or authorized officers.

| Category | Example deliverables | Priority |

| Equity and financing readiness | Review founder equity package, minority stockholder package, investor materials, securities exemption strategy, and future option pool structure | High |
| Commercial contracts | Draft or review customer, caregiver, vendor, partner, advisor, and platform agreements | High |
| Regulatory and compliance | Identify licensing, healthcare, homecare, privacy, employment, contractor, consumer protection, and state compliance issues | High |
| Fundraising support | Review fundraising documents, investor diligence materials, SAFE/note/equity structures, and disclosure materials | Medium |
| IP and confidentiality | Review IP assignment, confidentiality, invention assignment, contractor, and advisor IP controls | Medium |
| Risk management | Create legal risk register, insurance recommendations, and dispute/litigation response workflow | Medium |

## 4. Monthly Review

At least once per month during the 18-month vesting period, RJ and the Company will review:

- completed tasks;
- open tasks;
- blockers requiring Company input;
- upcoming priorities;
- funding commitment status;
- whether vesting conditions for the period have been satisfied.

## 5. Professional Limits

RJ is not required to guarantee results, take positions that RJ reasonably believes are improper, or perform work that would violate applicable legal ethics rules.

## 6. Company Cooperation

The Company will provide timely information, documents, access, approvals, and business decisions reasonably required for RJ to complete assigned tasks.
