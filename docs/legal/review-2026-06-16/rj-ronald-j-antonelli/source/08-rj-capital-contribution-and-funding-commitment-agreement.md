## 3. Character of Contributions

Unless the Board approves otherwise in writing before a payment is made:

- each payment is a capital contribution to the Company;
- no payment is a loan;
- no payment bears interest;
- no payment is automatically refundable;
- no payment entitles RJ to additional shares, options, warrants, SAFEs, notes, liquidation preference, or other securities.

## 4. Payment Method

RJ shall make payments by wire transfer, ACH, check, or another method approved by the Company. The Company shall provide payment instructions in writing.

## 5. Default

5.1 **Funding Default.** RJ is in funding default if a required installment remains unpaid for 10 business days after written notice from the Company.

5.2 **Effect of Default.** A funding default may:

- constitute a material default under the Legal Services and Strategic Advisor Agreement;
- pause vesting of unvested shares;
- trigger company repurchase rights for unvested shares;
- allow the Company to pursue any remedies available under the applicable agreements and law.

5.3 **No Automatic Forfeiture of Vested Shares.** A funding default does not automatically forfeit vested shares unless a separate written agreement expressly provides otherwise and counsel confirms enforceability.

## 6. No Waiver

The Company's acceptance of a late payment does not waive any prior or future default unless the Company expressly waives the default in writing.

## 7. Professional Responsibility

The parties acknowledge that RJ is a lawyer and that the Funding Commitment may be part of a business transaction between a lawyer and client. The parties shall sign a Professional Responsibility Disclosure and Consent before or concurrently with this Agreement.

## 8. Miscellaneous

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

8.2 **Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Company and RJ.

8.3 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
