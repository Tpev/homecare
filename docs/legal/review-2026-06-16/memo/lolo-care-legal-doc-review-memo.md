## Legal Reference Points

These documents are built around Delaware corporate-law concepts. Key provisions include DGCL Section 141 for board management, quorum, director voting, written consents, weighted director voting if in the charter, and director removal; DGCL Section 152 for board-authorized stock issuance and consideration; DGCL Section 158 for certificated and uncertificated shares; and DGCL Section 220 for inspection rights.
