## How To Use This List

The `Primary action` column tells the salesperson exactly what should happen first.

Verification note:

- This list was checked against official public-facing pages on April 4, 2026.
- Where a direct named contact was not clearly exposed on an official page, this sheet uses the main public line or website instead of guessing.
- For churches in particular, office lines and general inboxes are safer than assuming a ministry lead is still the right contact.

Definitions:

- `Call first`: call before showing up
- `Visit after call`: in-person drop-in after a positive or neutral call
- `Email after visit`: send materials same day after a live conversation
- `Meeting request`: request a specific sit-down or intro call
