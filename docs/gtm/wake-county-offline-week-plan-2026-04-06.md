# Wake County Offline Week Plan

Date prepared: April 4, 2026
Execution week: Monday, April 6, 2026 through Friday, April 10, 2026
Owner: Salesperson

## Weekly Objective

Secure trusted offline distribution in Wake County by getting HomeCare in front of families through senior centers, community centers, churches, and caregiver-serving organizations.

## Weekly KPI Scorecard

Track these numbers daily:

- physical stops completed
- calls completed
- real conversations with decision-makers
- approved flyer placements
- bulletin or newsletter submissions
- follow-up meetings booked
- talks or table opportunities booked
- partnership or referral conversations opened

Weekly target:

- 25 stops
- 40 outbound calls
- 15 real conversations
- 10 flyer placements
- 5 follow-up meetings
- 4 event, table, or speaking opportunities
- 3 bulletin or newsletter inclusions

## Non-Negotiables

Do these every day:

1. Start with calls before the first visit block.
2. Visit in person after a positive or neutral call.
3. Ask for more than a flyer.
4. Log every interaction before moving to the next stop.
5. Send all promised follow-up emails the same day.

## Materials Checklist

Bring every morning:

- 30 family flyers
- 15 church-version flyers
- 10 partner one-pagers
- business cards
- sign-up sheet
- clipboard
- pen pack
- table-top sign if a meeting or event is possible
- printed schedule with available talk dates
- CRM sheet or phone notes template

## Required Process At Every Stop

1. Ask who handles community materials, family resources, or senior programs.
2. Give the short HomeCare explanation.
3. Ask for the most relevant next step.
4. Leave only the right amount of material.
5. Capture contact name, role, and response.
6. Confirm next action before leaving.

## Ask Ladder

Ask in this order:

1. permission to leave flyers
2. permission for bulletin or newsletter inclusion
3. table at an event or resource fair
4. 5 to 10 minute educational talk
5. introduction to the right program, care, or pastoral staff
