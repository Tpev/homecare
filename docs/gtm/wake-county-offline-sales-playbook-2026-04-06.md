# Wake County Offline Sales Playbook

Date prepared: April 4, 2026
Use during week of April 6, 2026

## Core Message

"HomeCare helps Wake County families quickly find non-medical in-home support for an aging parent. We are especially useful after discharge, when families need daytime help, or when adult children are coordinating care from work or from out of town."

## What To Say First

Never start with:

"Can I leave flyers?"

Start with:

"Hi, I am with HomeCare. We help Wake County families find non-medical in-home support for an aging parent. Who handles family resource information or community materials here?"

## Quick Qualification

If the person seems open, ask:

- "Do families here ever ask about in-home support for an aging parent?"
- "Who would be the best person to speak with about family resources or caregiver support?"
- "Do you allow community resource materials, newsletter blurbs, or short educational talks?"

## Call Scripts

### Script 1: Senior center front desk

"Hi, I am calling from HomeCare. We help Wake County families find non-medical in-home support for an aging parent. I am trying to find out who handles community resource materials or family caregiver information for your center. Could you point me to the right person?"

If they ask what you want:

"We would love permission to leave a small set of family resource flyers and, if useful, talk with the right staff member about a short educational session for families or caregivers."

### Script 2: Senior center director or program lead

"We are building awareness in Wake County for families who suddenly need non-medical support at home for an aging parent. We already have caregiver supply locally, and we are trying to become a practical local resource rather than just another ad. Would you be open to letting us leave materials, submit a newsletter blurb, or discuss a short informational talk?"

### Script 3: Church office

"Hi, I am reaching out from HomeCare. We help Wake County families find non-medical support for an aging parent at home. I am looking for the person who handles member care, pastoral care, senior adult ministry, or church bulletin announcements. Who would that be?"

### Script 4: Pastor or pastoral care lead

"A lot of adult children in churches are quietly trying to coordinate help for an aging parent, especially after a health event or when work makes daily support hard. We would love to be a practical local resource. Would it be useful to leave materials, submit a bulletin blurb, or offer a short caregiver-focused session?"

### Script 5: Community center

"Hi, I am with HomeCare. We are a Wake County resource for families who need non-medical support at home for an aging parent. I wanted to ask who handles community bulletin materials or local family resource information for your center."

### Script 6: Nonprofit or referral organization

"We know you already support older adults and caregivers in Wake County. We are not asking for a hard referral right away. We would simply value an introductory conversation to explain where HomeCare may fit as a private-pay non-medical option when families need help quickly."

## Walk-In Script

Use this when entering a center, church office, or nonprofit office:

"Hi, I am with HomeCare. We help Wake County families find non-medical in-home support for an aging parent. I wanted to introduce us and ask who handles community resources, caregiver support information, or approved materials here."

If you meet the right person:

"Thank you for taking a minute. We are trying to become a useful local resource for families who need help at home. We would love to ask about three things: whether you allow approved materials, whether you have a bulletin or newsletter, and whether a short educational session would ever be useful for your audience."

## Objection Handling

### "We do not allow flyers."

Say:

"No problem. Do you ever share local resources through a newsletter, email, bulletin, or event table? That would still be very helpful."

### "We cannot endorse outside businesses."

Say:

"Understood. We are simply asking whether HomeCare can be listed as a local resource families may choose to explore, not as an endorsed provider."

### "Send me something by email."

Say:

"Absolutely. I will send a one-page summary and short blurb today. What is the best email for that, and when would be a good time for me to follow up?"

### "We already work with other care organizations."

Say:

"That makes sense. We may still be useful as another local non-medical option, especially for families looking for caregiver choice and faster access. Would it still be alright if I left a small set of materials or emailed a short overview?"

### "This is not a fit for us."

Say:

"Understood. Is there another person or organization in Wake County you think would be a better fit for this kind of family resource?"

## Close Lines

Use one of these:

- "Would it be alright if I left 10 flyers here?"
- "Could I email you a 75-word bulletin blurb today?"
- "Would it make sense to pencil in a 10-minute educational talk later this month?"
- "Who else should I speak with here before I go?"
- "Can I follow up with you on Tuesday?"

## Bulletin / Newsletter Blurb

## In-Person Behavior Rules

1. Dress professionally but not too formally.
2. Keep the first interaction under 3 minutes unless invited deeper.
3. Do not leave oversized stacks of flyers.
4. Always ask permission before placing anything.
5. Take a photo of every approved placement if allowed.
6. Record the contact immediately after leaving.

## Information To Capture In CRM

Every interaction must record:

- organization name
- contact name
- contact role
- phone
- email
- date and time
- whether reached by call or visit
- whether flyers were approved
- whether bulletin or newsletter was discussed
- whether event or talk was discussed
- next step
- follow-up date

## Best Offers To Lead With

Use these offers repeatedly:

- "A local family resource flyer"
- "A short bulletin or newsletter blurb"
- "A 10-minute caregiver education talk"
- "A resource table at an existing event"

## The Real Goal

A flyer placement is good.

A relationship is better.

A repeating channel is best.
