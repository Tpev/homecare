Payment Terms and Cancellation Policy

This Payment Terms and Cancellation Policy (“Payment Policy”) governs payment processing, fees, cancellations, refunds, and financial transactions that may occur through the LoLo Care, Inc. (“LoLo”) platform (the “Platform”).
This Policy forms part of and is incorporated into the LOLO Platform Terms of Service. If there is a conflict between this Policy and the Terms of Service, the Terms of Service control unless this Policy specifically states otherwise.
## 1. Payment Processing
Payments made through the Platform may be processed through third-party payment processors selected by LOLO in its discretion, including Stripe or similar financial service providers.
By using payment features of the Platform, you agree that:
- payment processors may collect, process, and store payment information;
- you may be required to agree to the processor’s terms of service and privacy policy;
- LOLO does not store full payment card numbers or banking credentials when third-party processors handle payment transactions; and
- financial transactions are subject to the policies and operational rules of the payment processor.
LOLO may change payment processors at any time.

LOLO may charge various fees for use of the Platform, which may include:
- service fees
- marketplace facilitation fees
- subscription fees
- processing fees
- cancellation or no-show fees
- premium feature fees
- enterprise or organizational service fees
All fees will be disclosed on the Platform or through applicable agreements before being charged.
LOLO reserves the right to modify fee structures, provided changes are communicated through the Platform or through updated policies.
## 3. Pricing for Services
Pricing, service rates, fees, and payout amounts may be set, standardized, or displayed by LOLO through the Platform or established in applicable booking flows or separate agreements.
Where the Platform permits service providers or organizations to propose or select pricing, those amounts remain subject to Platform rules and disclosure before confirmation.
- Users are responsible for reviewing and agreeing to applicable rates, fees, and payout terms before confirming or accepting a booking or service request.
- Unless a booking displays a different legacy or separately agreed price, Family care is $30 per worked hour and a $1 per worked hour processing fee is added, for a $31 per worked hour Family total.
- Unless a booking displays a different legacy or separately agreed price, caregiver gross earnings are $27 per worked hour before processing fees.
## 4. Authorization and Payment Collection
When a user confirms a service request or booking through the Platform, the user may be required to authorize payment.
Authorization may include:
- pre-authorization holds
- advance charges
- deposits
- milestone payments
- final payment following service completion
Payment timing may vary depending on the workflow, service type, or marketplace configuration.
## 5. Caregiver or Provider Payouts
Where the Platform facilitates payments to caregivers or service providers:
- payouts may be issued through third-party processors;
- payout timing may vary depending on processor schedules, fraud checks, or compliance reviews;
- LOLO may delay or withhold payouts where necessary for dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or policy enforcement.
- the caregiver processing fee is the actual payment-processing fee recorded by Stripe on successful Family charges associated with that visit, including a successful additional charge when worked time exceeds the original authorization;
- refund costs, dispute fees, and optional instant-payout fees are not included in the caregiver processing-fee deduction described above; and
- a transfer from LOLO to a caregiver's Stripe balance is not the same as a payout from Stripe to the caregiver's bank account. Stripe controls bank-payout timing and availability.
Caregivers and providers are responsible for:
- accurate payout information
- tax reporting
- compliance with applicable laws.

## 7. Cancellations
The Platform may permit users to cancel scheduled services.
Cancellation rules may vary depending on the workflow, but generally:
- cancellations made sufficiently in advance may not incur fees;
- late cancellations may result in partial charges; and
- repeated cancellations may result in account restrictions.
Specific cancellation windows may be defined within the booking workflow.
## 8. No-Shows
If a user fails to appear for a scheduled service without cancelling in accordance with the Platform rules, the user may be charged a no-show fee in addition to the cost for the service.
No-show policies are intended to protect the time and availability of service providers.
LOLO may determine the appropriate fee structure or delegate fee policies to marketplace participants depending on how the Platform is configured.
## 9. Rescheduling
Users may be permitted to reschedule services through the Platform when functionality allows.
Rescheduling may be subject to:

- timing rules
- cancellation windows
Rescheduling requests made outside permitted windows may be treated as cancellations.
