LoLo Care Inc
Acceptable Use and Safety Policy
Effective Date: March 13, 2026 | Last Updated: March 13, 2026
This Acceptable Use and Safety Policy (“Safety Policy”) establishes standards of conduct for all users of the LoLo Care platform (the “Platform”), including clients, caregivers, providers, partners, and visitors (collectively, “Users”).
This Policy is incorporated into the LoLo Platform Terms of Service and related agreements. By using the Platform, you agree to comply with this Safety Policy.
1. Purpose
The purpose of this Policy is to maintain a safe, respectful, and lawful environment for all participants using the Platform.
Users must conduct themselves in a manner that protects the safety, dignity, privacy, and legal rights of others.
2. Prohibited Conduct
Users may not engage in any conduct that is unlawful, abusive, harmful, unsafe, or disruptive to the Platform or its participants.
Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:
harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination
violence or threats of violence
stalking or unwanted contact
sexual harassment or exploitation
abuse of elderly or vulnerable individuals
fraud, deception, or impersonation
theft or property damage
illegal activity
misuse of personal information
attempts to bypass Platform safeguards
3. Discrimination and Harassment
LoLo prohibits discrimination or harassment based on protected characteristics, including but not limited to:
race
ethnicity
national origin
religion
gender
sexual orientation
disability
age
Users must treat each other with respect and professionalism.
4. Unsafe Environments
Users must not request or provide services in environments that pose a clear safety risk.
Examples include:
violent or threatening situations
illegal activities
hazardous conditions
presence of weapons or dangerous materials where safety cannot be maintained
Providers and clients are encouraged to decline or stop services if they feel unsafe.
5. Protection of Vulnerable Individuals
The Platform may involve interactions with elderly individuals, people with disabilities, or others requiring assistance.
Users must not engage in:
neglect
exploitation
financial abuse
emotional abuse
physical abuse
Any suspected abuse should be reported immediately to appropriate authorities where required by law.
6. Prohibited Services
Users may not use the Platform to request or provide services that are:
illegal
dangerous
outside the scope permitted by law
medical or clinical services unless explicitly permitted by law and by Platform policy
The Platform is intended primarily for non‑clinical support services.
7. Privacy and Personal Information
Users must respect the privacy of others.
Users may not:
collect personal data without permission
share private information publicly
misuse sensitive information obtained through the Platform
Information obtained through the Platform should only be used for legitimate purposes related to services.
8. Fraud and Platform Abuse
Users may not:
create fake accounts
manipulate reviews or ratings
misrepresent qualifications
bypass Platform fees or payment systems
attempt to hack or disrupt the Platform
Such actions may result in immediate account suspension.
9. Reporting Safety Concerns
Users are encouraged to report safety concerns or policy violations.
Reports may include:
unsafe behavior
harassment or discrimination
fraud or impersonation
abuse of vulnerable individuals
Reports may be submitted through the Platform or by contacting LoLo.
10. Investigations
LoLo may investigate reported incidents.
Investigations may include:
reviewing communications
requesting additional information
restricting account access
LoLo may cooperate with law enforcement where appropriate.
11. Enforcement
Violations of this Safety Policy may result in:
warnings
account suspension
account termination
removal of content
referral to law enforcement
LoLo reserves the right to take action to protect the safety of the Platform and its users.
12. Emergency Situations
The Platform is not an emergency service.
If an emergency occurs, users should contact local emergency services immediately.
13. Changes to This Policy
LoLo may update this Safety Policy periodically.
Updated versions will be posted on the Platform with a revised effective date.
Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
