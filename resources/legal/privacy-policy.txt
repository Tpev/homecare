LoLo Care Inc
Privacy Policy
Effective Date: March 13, 2026 | Last Updated: June 20, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how LoLo Care Inc (“LoLo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects personal information when you access or use our websites, mobile applications, software, communication tools, marketplaces, scheduling tools, messaging tools, and related products and services (collectively, the “Platform”).
This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Platform Terms of Service and any supplemental agreements, notices, or consents that may apply to specific products, workflows, users, or services.
Important: This Privacy Policy is intended as a business draft for review and customization by qualified legal counsel. Because the Platform may involve caregiving, scheduling, communication, operational workflows, payments, and healthcare-adjacent use cases, this Policy should be reviewed for compliance with applicable privacy, consumer protection, and health-information laws in each jurisdiction where the Platform operates.
1. Scope
This Privacy Policy applies to personal information we collect:
through the Platform;
when you create an account or profile;
when you submit forms, requests, or inquiries;
when you communicate with us or with other users through the Platform;
when you participate in scheduling, coordination, payment, verification, or support workflows;
when you subscribe to communications from us;
when you visit our website; and
when we otherwise interact with you in connection with the Platform.
This Privacy Policy does not apply to:
information processed solely on behalf of an enterprise, provider, partner, or customer where LoLo acts only as a service provider or processor under a separate agreement;
third-party websites, services, software, processors, or integrations not controlled by LoLo; or
offline services or activities not connected to the Platform, except where this Policy expressly says otherwise.
2. Categories of Information We Collect
Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect the following categories of information.
2.1 Information You Provide Directly
We may collect information you provide directly to us, including:
name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and other contact details;
account credentials and login information;
profile information, preferences, service interests, availability, qualifications, credentials, experience, and biographical details;
business, employer, facility, agency, or organizational information;
messages, support requests, uploaded files, notes, reviews, forms, submissions, and communications;
scheduling details, appointment preferences, service requests, and workflow data;
billing details, payment-related information, and transaction records;
identity verification materials, documents, certifications, licenses, and onboarding information; and
any other information you choose to provide through the Platform.
2.2 Information Collected Automatically
When you access or use the Platform, we may automatically collect certain information, including:
device type, browser type, operating system, IP address, and unique device identifiers;
usage data, session activity, clicks, navigation paths, referring pages, dates and times of access, and feature interaction data;
approximate geolocation derived from IP address;
cookies, pixels, tags, scripts, SDKs, and similar tracking technologies; and
diagnostic, analytics, security, and performance data.
2.3 Information From Third Parties
We may receive information from third parties, including:
payment processors;
identity verification, background check, or credentialing providers;
analytics providers and advertising partners;
customer relationship management, communications, scheduling, and support tools;
enterprise customers, partners, agencies, facilities, or referral sources;
social login providers or authentication providers, if enabled; and
publicly available sources or lawful third-party data sources.
2.4 Sensitive and Healthcare-Adjacent Information
Depending on how the Platform is implemented, users may submit information that is sensitive, caregiving-related, operationally sensitive, or health-related. This may include information concerning care needs, scheduling constraints, service preferences, mobility or support needs, household context, operational workflows, or related details.
LoLo does not intend for users to submit medical records, full clinical charts, diagnoses, treatment plans, insurance numbers, or other regulated health information unless expressly authorized as part of a specific workflow and supported by appropriate agreements, notices, and safeguards.

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

to support coordination, communication, scheduling, matching, workflow management, and other Platform functions;
to verify identity, eligibility, credentials, and onboarding status;

to send account notices, security notices, transactional communications, reminders, alerts, and operational updates;
to provide customer service, troubleshooting, and technical support;
to analyze usage, trends, adoption, and performance;
to personalize content, features, workflows, and user experiences;
to detect, investigate, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, safety incidents, policy violations, and illegal activity;
to protect users, LoLo, and third parties;
to enforce our agreements, policies, and legal rights;
to comply with legal obligations, regulatory requirements, lawful requests, and dispute-related needs; and
to send marketing communications where permitted by law and subject to available opt-out rights.
4. Legal Bases for Processing
Where required by applicable law, we process personal information on one or more of the following bases:
your consent;
performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract;
compliance with legal obligations;
protection of vital interests;
public interest, where applicable; and
our legitimate interests, including operating the Platform, improving services, preventing fraud, securing systems, supporting users, and conducting reasonable business operations, except where overridden by rights that require a different standard.
5. How We Share Information
We may share personal information in the following circumstances:
5.1 With Other Users or Participants
If a Platform workflow requires interaction, coordination, scheduling, matching, or communication between users, we may share relevant profile, scheduling, request, communication, and workflow information with other users or authorized participants as reasonably necessary for the Platform to function.
5.2 With Service Providers
We may share information with vendors, contractors, and service providers that perform services on our behalf, including providers of hosting, infrastructure, analytics, communications, customer support, verification, background checks, scheduling, payments, email delivery, text messaging, document storage, security, and professional services.
Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in consent, and text messaging consent records are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may share mobile information and SMS consent records only with service providers that help us deliver messages, operate the Platform, comply with law, protect users, or complete communications you request, and those providers may use the information only to provide services to LoLo.
5.3 With Payment Processors
If payment features are enabled, payment-related information may be collected, processed, and stored by third-party processors under their own terms and privacy policies. LoLo may receive limited transaction details, billing status, payout status, and related records necessary to support the Platform.
5.4 With Enterprise Customers, Partners, or Organizations
If you use the Platform through or in connection with an employer, facility, agency, enterprise customer, referral partner, or other organization, we may share information with that organization consistent with the applicable workflow, relationship, permissions, and any governing agreements.
5.5 For Legal, Safety, and Compliance Reasons
We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:
comply with law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request;
enforce our terms, policies, and agreements;
protect the rights, property, safety, or security of LoLo, users, or others;
investigate fraud, abuse, harassment, unlawful conduct, or security incidents; or
respond to emergencies or urgent situations where disclosure is appropriate or required by law.
5.6 Business Transfers
We may disclose or transfer information in connection with an actual or proposed merger, financing, acquisition, sale of assets, bankruptcy, reorganization, or other corporate transaction.
5.7 With Your Direction or Consent
We may share information with third parties when you direct us to do so or otherwise consent.
6. Cookies, Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking Technologies
We and our third-party providers may use cookies, pixels, tags, web beacons, SDKs, scripts, local storage, and similar technologies to:
keep you signed in;
remember settings and preferences;
analyze traffic, engagement, and Platform performance;
understand usage patterns and feature adoption;
improve security and site performance;
support communications, analytics, and marketing activities;
measure campaign performance and audience behavior; and
help us improve user experience and business operations.
For caregiver pre-launch marketing pages, LoLo may also use first-party analytics events and a first-party anonymous identifier cookie to measure unique visits, understand conversion to signup, and improve onboarding flow quality. These records may include hashed IP and hashed user-agent values, URL/referrer context, and campaign parameters (such as UTM tags) for operational analytics purposes.
The Platform may use analytics tools, advertising technologies, retargeting tools, and similar services provided by third parties. These tools may collect information about your device, browser, IP address, activity on the Platform, pages viewed, clicks, session information, and referral sources.
The Platform may also use session replay or similar diagnostic tools to help us understand usability issues, troubleshoot technical problems, improve workflows, and optimize the user experience. Where used, these tools are intended to support product improvement, operations, and security.
Depending on the jurisdiction and the way the Platform is configured, LoLo may also use cookie banners, consent management tools, or similar mechanisms to provide notice and obtain or record user consent for certain tracking activities.
You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings, device controls, or available consent preferences. Blocking certain cookies or tracking technologies may affect Platform functionality.
7. Communications Preferences
We may send you service-related and transactional communications, including account notices, verification messages, security alerts, reminders, scheduling updates, workflow notifications, and payment-related notices.
We may also send marketing or promotional communications where permitted by law. You may opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in those emails. You may not be able to opt out of service-related or legally required communications.
If we use text messaging, automated calls, prerecorded calls, or similar communications, those communications will be subject to applicable law, your permissions, and any separate notices or consent language presented to you. By providing a mobile number and opting in where required, you authorize LoLo to send text messages related to your account, care requests, caregiver activity, scheduling, support, and other Platform communications. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. You may opt out of non-essential SMS messages by replying STOP, and you may request help by replying HELP where supported.
LoLo does not share mobile information, text messaging opt-in data, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and for using appropriate care when submitting information through the Platform.
