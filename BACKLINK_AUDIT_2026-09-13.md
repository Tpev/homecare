# CareLoLo backlink and press-release audit

**Reviewed:** 13 September 2026. **Target:** carelolo.com. **Release:** the 3 September 2026 announcement of LoLo Care's home-care marketplace launch in North Carolina's Triangle region.

## Result

**Yes—the recent PR produced identifiable backlinks.** This audit verified **16 PR-related pages across 15 referring root domains** with clickable links leading to carelolo.com. Two additional articles mention carelolo.com as plain text but do not link directly to it.

Search Console currently displays **31 external links from six domains**, with none of those 15 PR domains in its visible domain table. The live PR footprint is therefore larger than the current Search Console sample shows. This is not a claim that Google has credited 15 new domains, or a measured net increase from a pre-PR backlink inventory.

Most checked placements use `nofollow` and distribute or adapt the same announcement. The confirmed benefit is publication, discoverability, and routes for referral traffic. A corresponding ranking or customer-acquisition gain has not been established.

### Findings that affect value

**Most qualifiers are known, and most are `nofollow`.** Of 16 verified pages, 11 have `nofollow` on the inspected links, two have links without `nofollow`/`sponsored`, and three have link attributes unavailable. For those three, rendered page content confirmed the anchors and destinations, but the browser's DOM attribute read did not return usable data. Their qualifiers are deliberately not inferred from other syndicated copies.

**Feedworthy points its canonical URL to Newsramp.** Its link lacks `nofollow`, but its canonical names the Newsramp version of the article. The original Newsworthy release and its curated page each have self-referencing canonicals; Newsramp's article is also self-referencing. A declared canonical is not proof of the version Google selected. Feedworthy should not be promoted as a separate guaranteed ranking gain.

**All sampled PR links lead to the homepage.** They do not link to a specific Raleigh, Durham, service, or booking page. Homepage links still provide a route into the service, but the release does not directly strengthen a varied set of landing pages through these links. Its inspected anchor text is mainly the brand, the website URL, and “Additional Information,” not a broad set of service keywords.

**The Newsfile tracking links work.** The two repeated links, `/redirect/any84uXrWK` and `/redirect/24baYUWbe2` on api.newsfilecorp.com, both reached the homepage. That supports referral usability. This browser check did not capture every intermediate HTTP status or establish how a crawler treats the redirect chain.

**Publication is not independent editorial endorsement.** The copies share the announcement, distribution sources, and boilerplate. Several adapted articles use Newsramp infrastructure or link back to the original release. StreetInsider also has a [separate Newsworthy version](https://www.streetinsider.com/Newsworthy/%2BLoLo%2BCare%2BLaunches%2BInnovative%2BHome%2BCare%2BMarketplace%2Bin%2BNorth%2BCarolina%E2%80%99s%2BTriangle%2BRegion%2B/27022943.html) explicitly described as paid press-release content rather than work by its news staff. That separate page was found in search but is not included in the 16 verified backlink-page count.

Do not ask paid-distribution publishers to remove link qualifications just to pass ranking credit. Google's guidance uses `sponsored` for advertising/paid placements and still accepts `nofollow`; ordinary `noopener`/`noreferrer` attributes serve different purposes and do not mean `nofollow`. No attribute configuration guarantees a ranking increase. [Google: qualifying outbound links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links). Google's spam policies also distinguish legitimate promotion from paid or optimized distributed links intended to manipulate rankings. [Google: link spam](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).

These are useful publication/mention evidence, but counting them as direct backlinks to carelolo.com would overstate delivery. A reasonable distributor follow-up is to request a clickable website address for reader usability, retaining appropriate paid-link qualifications.

The original Newsworthy page also lists social, podcast, archive, and newsletter distribution URLs. Those destinations were not counted without verifying their own links. MarketMinute provider-index results likewise were not counted as separate article backlinks. This avoids inflating the inventory with logos, feed listings, archive links, or duplicate hosts.

## 4. Did the PR improve traffic or rankings?

The earlier GA4 audit found two sessions each from newsfilecorp.com, newsworthy.ai, and streetinsider.com during 15 August–11 September: **six unfiltered attributed sessions in total** from those three sources. Those figures include the owner's France activity and may include checking the release. They do not establish six external prospects or capture all PR-assisted visits. GA4 recorded no business outcomes, so PR leads, bookings, revenue, and ROI remain unmeasured.

The backlink findings prove that distribution occurred. They do not isolate a ranking effect. There is no pre-release backlink export, no verified Google first-discovery date for the PR pages, and no clean PR-attributed conversion baseline. A dated release page establishes the publisher's stated publication date, not the date Google discovered or credited its link.

Evaluate future performance using France-excluded traffic, valid outcome events, and comparisons of branded versus non-branded search demand. Keep publisher referral visits separate from staff visits and link-checking activity. A distribution report's estimated audience is not measured readership or acquired customers.

## 5. Recommended next steps

1. **Request the distributor's full placement report.** Ask for exact article URLs, publication dates, live-link destinations, direct versus redirect routing, and the distinction between original reporting, syndicated copies, and plain-text mentions. Compare it against this verified inventory. No request was sent during this audit.
2. **Correct missing clickable links where appropriate.** FisherVista and HR Vendor News are specific examples. Ask for a usable link to the relevant care page while retaining the publisher's appropriate `nofollow`/`sponsored` treatment.
3. **Pursue relevant editorial coverage.** The next useful PR objective is a real Triangle-area story, partner resource, caregiver/community feature, or customer case study with original reporting and a relevant audience. Another large list of syndicated copies would mainly repeat the distribution already demonstrated here.
4. **Keep a dated backlink baseline.** Save this inventory and compare later live checks with Search Console's domain table and Latest links export. Count unique root domains and source pages, not repeated anchors, publisher logos, or the same feed under several paths. Record Google discovery dates separately from publisher dates when available.
5. **Measure business impact before judging PR spend.** Fix the Analytics outcome and owner-traffic issues already documented. Use consistent tracking for future controllable campaign links, and evaluate genuine referral sessions, qualified family inquiries, caregiver activation, and bookings alongside search visibility.

The audit found no basis for a disavow action. A domain name, a press-release qualifier, or a small sample is not sufficient evidence of harmful link manipulation.

## Method and limitations

This audit combined signed-in Search Console reports, public search discovery, live page inspection, anchor destinations/attributes, selected canonical/robots metadata, two redirect destination checks, and the earlier GA4 source report. It used no purchased backlink subscription, proprietary authority score, or fabricated estimated traffic.

The inventory is a **verified minimum**, not the full web's backlink graph. Public search can miss recent or unindexed pages. Links absent from the current Search Console sample can still exist and be known to Google. Conversely, a live link or a page appearing in search is not proof of ranking credit. Meta robots/canonical checks do not substitute for HTTP-header and robots.txt checks or Google's own selected canonical.

A Latest links CSV export was initiated, but no usable downloaded file was retrieved. The browser's security policy blocked access to its download-management page. No export contents or Google discovery dates were used in the conclusions. No settings, site content, publisher content, subscriptions, or outreach messages were changed.
